    Layout: the Now bar is always one row, showing the most important item: Needs you first, then Running, then the newest. It never grows. One-word states (Thinking, Offline, Reconnecting) are a centred pill that hugs its text. With more than one item, the count badge replaces the leading icon and a small grabber sits at the top edge of the row. The whole row is the button: tap it or swipe it up to bring up the Background activity sheet from the bottom. Approve and Decline in the row act on their own and do not open the sheet.
