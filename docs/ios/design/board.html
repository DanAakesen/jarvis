The Now bar is one row and shows the most urgent item: Needs you first, then Running. It hides when nothing is queued, running or waiting. With more than one item, a round count badge leads the row: amber with a ring and the number of Needs you items when any exist, otherwise the accent colour with the number of active items (Needs you, Running and Queued). Layout: the Now bar is always one row, showing the most important item: Needs you first, then Running, then the newest. It never grows. One-word states (Thinking, Offline, Reconnecting) are a centred pill that hugs its text. With more than one item, the count badge replaces the leading icon and a small grabber sits at the top edge of the row. The whole row is the button: tap it or swipe it up to bring up the Background activity sheet from the bottom. Approve and Decline in the row act on their own and do not open the sheet. A single item is a plain row with its icon, no badge and no grabber. Close the sheet with its X, a swipe down, a tap outside or Escape. Proposed

Web today: Thinking, tool calls (Running, Done, Refused, Failed), pending confirmations with Approve and Decline, and the task link. Queued, Clear and the sheet have no web equivalent.
