## North star
**Chaos in, structure out.** Dan throws in ideas, asks and loose thoughts. Jarvis files them, then follows up until each is moved forward or binned.

## 1. Inbox and follow-up loop (new, top)
- One long-lived session. No lost context.
- Capture every idea and ask, in any channel.
- Structure each: what and why, in free text. No fixed status values; the model judges.
- Follow up proactively: move on it, or bin it?
- **Decided:** lives in the vault (one note per item, inbox folder). Small rebuildable index for last nudge and thumbs up/down. GitHub only when it becomes real work.
- **Status:** design in progress in a cloud session (#1836). Diagram: `docs/inbox-loop/inbox-loop.png`.

## 2. Proactive Jarvis
- **Interrupt for:** reminders (always), stalled repos, prep or deadlines found in email and calendar, plan-versus-reality gaps (e.g. moved task date vs plan).
- **Welcome, unprompted:** feature ideas, design reviews, codebase analysis. Think outside the box.
- **Stay quiet:** routine email, unimportant calendar events.
- **Signals:** email, calendar, GitHub, Azure cost, health and training, banking, location.
- **Channels:** push, call, in-app. iMessage not possible.
- **Quiet hours:** none needed. Phone is on Do Not Disturb at night.
- **Feedback:** log every outreach; thumbs up/down, ideally on the push itself.
- **May act:** draft emails (never send), feature designs, codebase analysis, news on Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic tech.
- **Budget:** 100 DKK/month max.
- **Trust comes from:** outreach that adds value, sound repo decisions, quality autonomous actions.

## Design principle
- Model-driven by default: free-form, minimal fixed fields, the model's judgement decides, so quality grows as models improve. Deterministic logic only where exact results are required.
