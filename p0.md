## North star

  2. #1886: reach (GitHub, Azure, Workers), the brain loop, standing watches ("keep me updated on X").

## 2. What PI should do
- **Interrupt for:** reminders, stalled repos, prep or deadlines in email and calendar, plan-versus-reality gaps.
- **Welcome, unprompted:** feature ideas, design reviews, codebase analysis, news on Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic tech.
- **Stay quiet:** routine email, unimportant calendar events.
- **Channels:** a chat message always pushes to phone and Watch; call later; thumbs up/down on every outreach.
- **May act:** draft emails (never send), designs, analysis.
- **Budget:** 100 DKK/month for proactive work.
- **Built, off by default:** event pipeline (#1858) and the vault inbox (#1855, flags `inboxEnabled`, `inboxNudgesEnabled`). Both fold into PI.
